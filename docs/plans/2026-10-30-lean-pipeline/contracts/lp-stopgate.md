Unit: lp-stopgate

## Objective
Reduce the stop gate to one pending-review flag check plus the marker-forgery check (plan change 4, decisions D6 and D7, step "U8a lp-stopgate"). The rules, exactly:
- Under `reviewGating.mode: off` the gate exits 0.
- On a gated writer's SubagentStop, it honours the WIP sentinel. Otherwise it writes the flag, then blocks, naming the file, if any `*.pass`, `*.fail` or `*.blocked` in `<dot>/reviewed/` is newer than `<dot>/reviewed/.writer-dispatch-stamp`. The flag comes first: the loop-guarded stop after a block is allowed, so a flag written after the check would never be written (safety gap found after 7c92f937, orchestrator decision).
- On a reviewer SubagentStop, it clears all flags.
- On the main Stop, it blocks while a flag exists whose first line starts with neither `skip:` nor `defer:`.
- `dispatch-hygiene.sh` touches the stamp on every gated writer dispatch.
No version bump.
