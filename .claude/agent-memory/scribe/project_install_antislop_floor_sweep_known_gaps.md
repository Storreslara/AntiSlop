## Known gaps from unit install-antislop-floor-sweep (PASS 2026-09-24)

**Gap: No mechanical enforcement of scaffold-only mirror parity**

**Fact:** The unit fixed a real bug where `skills/install-antislop/SKILL.md` and its mirror `.claude/skills/install-antislop/SKILL.md` drifted out of sync, causing users on compatibility floor versions 2.1.178–2.1.247 to silently lose the `experimental.cacheTtl` frontmatter field during setup.

**Why:** The **scaffold-only mirror** mechanism (files copied ONLY at scaffold time and never regenerated by `--update`) creates an operational risk: parity drift is silent and can recur on any future edit to a source file in `skills/` unless the editor remembers to hand-sync the mirror.

**How to apply:** A future unit could add a `tests/validate.sh` check that diffs `.claude/skills/` against `skills/` and fails if they diverge, catching drift at merge-gate time rather than silently after shipping. This is a candidate follow-up, not required now (defer to future maintenance ticket).

**Current scope:** CONTEXT.md entry added for [[scaffold-only mirror]] glossary term, defining the concept and listing affected files (`.claude/skills/*` and their source paths). Operational implication documented: parity is enforced by code-review process, not mechanically.
